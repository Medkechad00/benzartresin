Most people stall on the same question, and it is rarely the price. It is that you are being asked to commit to an object that does not exist yet. No stock photograph, no size chart, nothing to stand next to in a showroom.

That discomfort is reasonable. Here is how the process actually runs, so you know what you are agreeing to at each point.

## What "made to order" really means

Every Benzart Resin piece starts as one specific board from one specific tree. We hold no finished inventory, and we cannot build the same design twice in the same way, because no two slabs are alike. Grain, the line of the live edge, the voids the resin fills — the timber decides those, not us.

So you are not really commissioning a product. You are commissioning a negotiation with a material that has its own opinions. A board can reveal a check or an inclusion the moment it is surfaced. The tables that work are the ones designed around those features instead of in spite of them.

## The four stages

**1. Consultation.** You describe the room, the footprint you can take, how many people need to sit, and what you are drawn to. We talk through species, resin treatment and base. Nothing is charged here, and if we are the wrong workshop for the piece we will say so.

**2. Design and quote.** We source three to five candidate slabs against your brief and photograph each one. You choose a board, and the quote is fixed against it. A 50% deposit holds both the slab and your place in the queue.

**3. Crafting.** The slab is dried and moisture-tested, flattened, stabilised, then poured. Deep pours go in layers to keep the exothermic reaction under control — rush that and the heat stays in the resin. Once fully cured the top is flattened again, then sanded and finished by hand through progressive grits.

**4. Crating and shipping.** The table is crated in ISPM 15 certified timber and handed to a freight partner. The balance is due before dispatch.

For realistic durations at each step, see [the full custom table timeline](/blog/from-consultation-to-shipping-timeline).

## What actually drives the price

Four things explain most of the gap between a $3,000 table and a $15,000 one:

- **The slab.** Width, length, thickness, species, figure. A single board wide enough to yield a 100cm top without joining is dramatically rarer than two narrower boards, and priced accordingly.
- **Resin volume.** Resin is sold by weight. A wide river through a thick top can take many litres, and the deeper the pour the longer it sits curing.
- **Finish complexity.** A clear pour on a flat top is straightforward. Pigment layering, suspended elements and [sculpted or textured surfaces](/blog/sculpted-and-textured-resin-surfaces) all add bench hours.
- **Base and destination.** Steel bases are fabricated to order, and freight on a heavy, fragile, crated object varies enormously by country.

## Choosing the materials

Species sets the character of the whole piece: colour, how the grain behaves, hardness, how it ages. [Walnut, olive and maple](/blog/choosing-your-wood-walnut-vs-olive-vs-maple) each read differently under resin and under light.

Resin is the other half of the decision. A clear pour behaves like water and shows everything beneath it. Opaque pigment behaves like stone and hides the underside of the live edge completely. [Resin colours and finishes](/blog/resin-customization-colors-and-finishes) covers the palette and what each treatment does to the piece visually.

## Getting it to you

We ship worldwide. Two things are worth knowing early. All our crating timber carries ISPM 15 certification, the phytosanitary standard that stops wooden packaging being refused or destroyed at a border. And import duty is assessed by your country, not by us, so it is never inside our quote. [International shipping and handling](/blog/international-shipping-and-handling) covers what to expect on arrival.

## Before you get in touch

Have three things roughly settled: the largest footprint your room can genuinely take, the seating count you need, and a budget range. Those constrain the slab search far more than any aesthetic preference, and they let us tell you quickly whether what you want is possible.

Everything else — species, colour, edge treatment, base — we work out together.
